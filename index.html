About FreeBiz Tools & Privacy Architecture

Transparent documentation • Architecture • Privacy guarantee

What is FreeBiz Tools?

FreeBiz Tools is an open, client-side utility suite built for independent entrepreneurs, shopkeepers, craftspeople, and mobile service providers. It delivers everyday utilities — QR codes, social profile bios, e-commerce product copy, and sales receipts — without requiring accounts, monthly subscriptions, or cloud uploads.

100% Client-Side Engine Zero Tracking / Telemetry Zero Paid APIs Works in Airplane Mode No Credit Card Ever

Privacy & Data Architecture

Every tool operates strictly inside your browser's local sandbox:

  • Zero Remote Transmission: When you generate a QR code, type customer names, or calculate an invoice, no data is transmitted over the internet.
  • Zero Cookies & Storage Leaks: We do not set tracking cookies. The only browser storage used is your optional offline QA checklist saved in your device's private localStorage.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No tracking pixels, Google Analytics, or social widgets are loaded.

Supported Features & Known Limitations

  • QR Codes: Generates ISO-compliant QR codes up to standard version capacities. Supports PNG and SVG vector formats. Low-contrast color pairings display warning alerts to safeguard scanning reliability.
  • Bios & Descriptions: Template-driven algorithms. These do not call external generative AI APIs, guaranteeing zero usage bills, zero API key exposure, and instantaneous offline execution.
  • Invoices / Receipts: Instant calculation and printable slips. Designed for day-to-day point-of-sale customer receipts and internal records; not a legally certified tax filing invoice.
