Testing on Android Phones

  • Open in Chrome: Open the Google Chrome app on your Android phone and scan or paste the URL above.
  • Thumb Ergonomics: Test tapping the bottom navigation bar with one thumb. Notice all buttons have large 44px+ touch hitboxes.
  • File Downloads: Tap "Download PNG" or "Save TXT" — Android will save the files directly to your device's Downloads folder.
  • Clipboard Test: Tap "Copy Bio" or "Copy Text Slip" — you will see a green confirmation toast, and the text will be immediately ready to paste in WhatsApp, Notes, or Instagram.
2

Add to Home Screen (Web App)

  • On Android (Chrome): Tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) in the top right corner > tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  • On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button (square with arrow) > scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • Launch Fullscreen: Open it from your phone's home screen icon. It functions like a native, lightning-fast offline business toolbox.
3

Testing via Desktop Browser DevTools

  • Open DevTools: Press F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I (Mac: Cmd+Option+I).
  • Toggle Device Mode: Click the phone/tablet icon or press Ctrl+Shift+M.
  • Select Devices: Test on Samsung Galaxy S20 Ultra, Google Pixel 7, and iPhone 14 Pro.
  • Verify Layout: Check that there is zero horizontal scroll, text does not overflow, and inputs are easily readable.
4

Airplane Mode & Privacy Verification

  • Turn on Airplane Mode: After opening the page, switch your phone to Airplane Mode (or toggle "Offline" in DevTools Network tab).
  • Test Generators: Generate new QR codes, create bios, and calculate invoices — notice everything continues to work 100% offline!
  • Zero Data Leaks: Open the Network tab. Zero requests are sent when you type, generate, or download. Your business data remains completely private.
